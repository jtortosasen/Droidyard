# Known limits

One register of what is open: limits the code has on purpose, claims not yet verified,
work deferred, and questions the documents leave open. This register is the only home of open
items: documents do not keep their own open-question or to-verify sections. `Source` is
provenance: the ADR, plan or research note that raised the item, or the area document
when nothing else did. Seeded at
milestone 1n's close from every plan's §Unverified, §Not in this milestone and in-part
acceptance items, the ADRs' consequences, the documents' open questions and
[`mvp.md`](mvp.md) §Out of scope; items closed before 1n are not listed. **Each
milestone's close updates it**: new open items are added, and an item closed is kept with
the milestone that closed it in `Closed by`. Rows are ordered by Area, then Kind (limit,
unverified, deferred, open question). "planned: 1o/1p" names the plan that means to
close an item.

`Work` names, on an open row, the work that would close it (owner, 2026-10-17), so a
milestone's plan can gather the rows it closes on the way and the board groups them. It is
empty, a milestone (`2p`), or one of:

- `C1` to `C6`: the capability of that name in [`beyond.md`](../vision/beyond.md);
- `git`: the rest of Git, the UI's other writes and the editor's Git marks;
- `v1-code`, `v1-test`, `v1-device`, `v1-sdk`: the [v1 additions](mvp.md#beyond-the-mvp),
  code intelligence, `build.test`, the device and emulator extras, SDK provisioning;
- `distribution`, `platforms`: distribution beyond the installer; Linux, Intel Macs, Windows;
- `gradle`: the build sidecar's robustness, its unrecorded forms and its unmeasured costs;
- `api`: the capability API's and MCP's surface (envelope, events, schema, protocol features);
- `agents`: `init`, the hooks, the Sessions panel and each client's support;
- `files`: the file capabilities, the editor and the trash beyond what ships;
- `logcat`: `device.logs`, `device.watchLogs` and the Logcat panel;
- `policy`: the configuration, the policy and trust, and their UI;
- `device-check`: a level-5 pass on device facts proven on one image or not at all;
- `tests`: load-sensitive tests and the suite's time, as 2v did;
- `quick`: a small defect, fixable at any time without a plan;
- `review`: the owner's next review round of the UI;
- `trial`: a trial with a real client, browser or device that the owner runs.

An empty `Work` is a limit or check no planned work closes; it is taken up when it hurts.
